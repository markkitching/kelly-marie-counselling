About Kelly

Trained to listen. Here to help.

I'm Kelly Marie, a BACP-registered psychotherapist based in Leeds with over a decade of experience supporting people through life's most challenging moments.

My training spans person-centred, integrative and trauma-informed approaches, allowing me to work flexibly with each person I meet. I believe therapy should feel collaborative, warm and entirely free of judgement.

I work from a private, comfortable therapy room in Leeds as well as online, and I offer appointments during evenings and weekends to fit around your life.

How I Work

Therapeutic Approach

I draw from several evidence-based traditions, blending them thoughtfully to meet your unique needs.

Person-Centred

You are the expert on your own life. Rooted in Carl Rogers' humanistic tradition, this approach places your values, strengths and lived experience at the heart of everything we do together.

Integrative

No single theory fits every person. I weave together elements of CBT, psychodynamic thinking and mindfulness-based practice, tailoring the approach to what works best for you.

Trauma-Informed

Recognising how past experiences shape present-day responses, I work at your pace, creating the safety needed to gently explore and begin to heal trauma without ever overwhelming you.
