About Kelly

Qualified. Experienced. Here to help.

I'm Kelly Marie, a BACP-registered psychotherapist based in Leeds with over a decade of clinical experience working with individuals, couples, young people and organisations.

My practice is grounded in psychodynamic theory — drawing on how early relational experiences and unconscious patterns shape our present-day emotional lives. Working alongside integrative and trauma-informed approaches, I offer a thoughtful, evidence-based service tailored to your clinical needs.

I work from a private therapy room in Leeds and online, with appointments available during evenings and weekends to accommodate professional and personal schedules.

Methodology

Therapeutic Approach

Grounded in evidence-based practice, I draw from several clinical traditions to provide the most effective support for your individual presentation.

Psychodynamic

Grounded in the understanding that present difficulties are shaped by unconscious processes and early relational experiences. Psychodynamic therapy creates space to explore patterns, defences and emotional histories that may be driving current distress.

Integrative

Drawing on CBT, humanistic and systemic frameworks alongside the psychodynamic foundation, I tailor the clinical approach to what the evidence and your individual presentation calls for — not a single rigid model.

Trauma-Informed

Specialist training in trauma ensures sessions are conducted with safety, sensitivity and clinical rigour. Work proceeds at your pace, with careful attention to the impact of traumatic experience on the nervous system and relational patterns.
